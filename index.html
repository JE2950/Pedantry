Welcome, Young Person 4471

Someone heard you say "less biscuits" at lunch. They reported you. You have been sent to the Pedantico Institute for Young People Who Wish to Speak Properly, three miles under the sea, where nobody says "could of" and gets away with it.

Each department is run by a member of staff: a butler, a duchess, a professor and worse. Each one has a test. Pass the test and they stamp your form. When every lamp above the lift at the end of the hall is green, you may leave.

Dr Pedant is watching. He is always watching. He has nothing else to do.
